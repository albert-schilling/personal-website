Featured project

Bardery In active development

AI-generated, illustrated stories where the reader decides what happens next.

View on GitHub

The idea

Bardery writes and illustrates a story part by part, like an illustrated children's book. Each part ends in a choice: the reader picks one of three paths or describes their own, and Bardery continues from there. Readers can go back to any choice to branch off a new storyline, or have the story read aloud.

The challenge

Text, illustrations and narration have to stay consistent across a branching story, suit the reader's age from toddlers to young adults, work in nine languages, and run entirely in the EU. Bardery is also a public reference for the engineering a production AI app needs around its features.

Engineering highlights

Multi-modal generation

Story text streams to the app as it's generated. Illustrations and narration follow as background jobs with retries and fallbacks. Hero portraits and earlier illustrations go in as reference images to keep characters consistent.

Child-safety guardrails

Each age band has its own content policy. An LLM classifier decides whether a reader's free text is fine, should be gently reinterpreted or must be blocked. Content filters run at the strictest level on every model call.

Evals

A custom harness scores story scenarios per age band and language, including a red-team set. Deterministic checks plus an LLM judge calibrated against human labels, compared against a baseline.

Semantic search

Stories are embedded and searchable by meaning with pgvector, in the same Postgres that holds the job queue: one place to back up, one place to delete a family's data from.

Privacy by design

All hosting and every AI model run in the EU. No story or prompt content reaches telemetry, and the app has no trackers.

Cloud and delivery

Azure Container Apps that scale to zero, provisioned with Terraform. CI runs lint, type, test, migration, infrastructure and security checks before staged releases.
